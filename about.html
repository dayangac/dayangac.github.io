Emre Dayangac works on design environments and geometry processing: the tools people use to design and fabricate, and the geometry underneath them. He is studying Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Edinburgh, and is a member of the IdeaLab Design Environments Group.

His main project is Traceform, which unfolds 3D forms into flat patterns and routes circuits across their signed folds. Other notable work includes Otto, an accessible parametric design environment, and Parametrix, a platform for learning parametric design.

He previously did research in human-computer interaction.
