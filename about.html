Emre Dayangac is a rising senior at Hisar School in Istanbul, Turkey. As a prominent member IdeaLab Fablab, Emre leads research initiatives focusing on digital fabrication and human-computer interaction. He has developed various tools to teach individuals computer aided design processes such a parametric designing a program which created interactions between augmented reality (AR) and automata mechanical systems, created an quadruped ROS starter kit to be an introductory material for K12 to learn coding, electronics and ROS.

Emre is planning to study Computer Science at university, where he hopes to further explore the intersection of digital fabrication, theoretical computer science, human-centered design, and machine design. His multidisciplinary approach bridges software development, mechanical engineering, and interactive design to create innovative solutions for future computing paradigms.
